GPU-107: RCCL tuner configuration on MI355X, the pages that matter

Verdict: for single-node inference the tuning job is already done by the vendor stack, twice: SGLang's custom all-reduce handles every all_reduce below 64 MiB before RCCL sees it, and inside RCCL 2.30 AMD's direct all-reduce (DDA) handles the same range before any tuner is consulted. Above 64 MiB RCCL's default is already the optimum. The pages below carry the evidence; everything else in this folder is working material.

  • Results from rccl-tests
    Rules validated per collective and node count (6 collectives, 1-5 nodes, RCCL 2.27.7), with the full accept/reject audit: 395 rules judged, 120 kept.
  • In-model results on the current stack
    3 models, 6 traffic modes, 5 arms on RCCL 2.30.4. SGLang as shipped > RCCL as shipped (DDA) > our conf > no tuner, in every table.
  • Final single-node campaigns
    Above 64 MiB in rccl-tests and in the live models: nothing beats the default. Our conf plus AMD's rules removes the miss penalty. The conf loads through RCCL's built-in tuner, no plugin needed.
  • Why stock SGLang beats tuned RCCL
    The layers that can execute an all_reduce on one node and who wins, with an interactive step-by-step all-reduce walkthrough (4 or 8 GPUs).
  • RCCL's DDA all-reduce, from source
    One-shot and two-shot kernels, the GPU barrier, IPC handle exchange, XGMI, launch geometry; why a tuner never sees these calls.
  • How we search
    Grid vs racing vs Optuna vs random, replayed on the same measured data. Racing wins.

    Process A (rccl-tests sweep to proven conf) and Process B (live model A/B), files by role, design choices and why.
