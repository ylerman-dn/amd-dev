alltoall never reaches RCCL's collective tuner: it is decomposed into p2p send/recv tasks at enqueue, so NCCL_ALGO/NCCL_PROTO requests are no-ops, -A 1 prints N/A, and the tuner plugin cannot apply per-size rules. The search therefore sweeps the channel request only ('P2P/-/N'), the deployable artifact is one global NCCL_MIN/MAX_NCHANNELS value, and the A/B validates that env setting against the untouched default. Executed channels are read from the debug logs' 'p2p channels' line.
