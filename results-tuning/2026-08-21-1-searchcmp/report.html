Caveats

  • Node sets differ per grid — within-grid comparisons only.
  • broadcast/reduce 1n triage curve is from node 8, their grids from node 6 (only 1n default curve available).
  • all_gather below 16M/8M is Direct-substituted; conf covers RING sizes only.
  • Only broadcast/reduce 2n/3n winners are live-A/B-validated; the rest are unvalidated grid winners.
  • Optuna medians are its oracle-best stopping point (it cannot know when to stop in production).
