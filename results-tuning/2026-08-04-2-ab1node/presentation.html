RCCL default vs tuned config

A 12-rule tuner config measured against stock RCCL across 18 message sizes. 8 of 12 rules hold up — large gains from 4K to 1M. The other 4 do not, and one of them is a genuine landmine that costs 66% of peak bandwidth. ▲ The one rule you must not ship

allreduce 64M–512M → -65.7% at 512M

Four sizes, all four badly regressed (-65.7% to -63.4%), P(sup) 0.00. This single rule is what drags the whole-sweep average to -37.2%. It is the reason the config is validated rule-by-rule instead of shipped whole.

Reading of the evidence (mechanism, not a logged fact): the requested channels=48 lands where WarpSpeed would otherwise have expanded 56 base channels by ×4. 48 ÷ 4 = 12, which is exactly the number that appears in the selection column, and 64M–512M is the only range in the sweep where the selected channel count differs from the requested one. That is consistent with the request being consumed as the post-multiplier total, starving the collective of roughly 56565656 → 48 channels. Confirming it needs a channel-span read, not this column.

Measurement
  • 1 node, amd-mi355x-7, 8 ranks × 1 GPU. All results from this one node; nothing multi-node here.
  • RCCL 2.28.3-develop:2e42aa8, rccl-tests 2.17.9-develop:2e42aa8.
  • 7 repeats per arm, interleaved default/config so drift cannot favour either arm.
  • In-place busbw, median of 7. 0 incorrect elements across all 252 data rows.
Verification
  • Deployed through the RCCL v4 tuner plugin. Plugin firing checked per run: TUNER/Plugin: Using DN-TUNER (v4) in 56/56 config per-rank logs, and no DN-TUNER line in any of the 56 default logs.
  • Selection verified per size from the run’s own algo/proto/channel columns; identical across all 7 repeats at every size in both arms.
  • Keep threshold: P(sup) ≥ 0.95 and gain > 2.0%.
  • Both arms ran under identical NCCL_DEBUG=INFO→file logging, which is measured to be free (mean −0.06%), so the comparison is not biased by it.

Two things this page does not claim. First, these busbw numbers are not comparable to the sweep-measured ones: the sweep and this A/B reach the GPUs by different launch paths (mpirun vs srun), which shifts absolute bandwidth. Every comparison here is between the two arms of this A/B only, which were launched identically — the A/B is self-contained. Second, at the smallest sizes busbw is printed to two decimals, so one least-significant digit is worth about 3.1% at 4K. The gains there (+12.5% at 4K) are several digits wide and survive that limit, but the precision floor is real and the small-size percentages should not be read past one decimal.
