Reading the data: with concurrency 32, steady-state decode does one all_reduce per layer per step over the whole batch: 32 tokens × hidden_size × 2 B. That single size carries ~98% of hits for llama8b and qwen235b (262144 B → R6) and for qwen30b (131072 B → top edge of range rule R5). Single-token (ramp-up/drain) decode and 16 K-token prefill chunks make up the rest. gpt-oss (hidden 2880) emits 5760 B decode and 184320 B batched decode — neither matches any rule; only its prefill chunks (k×5760, k=12–22) fall into R5's 64–128 K range, and that alone coincided with a −1.7% throughput regression.
