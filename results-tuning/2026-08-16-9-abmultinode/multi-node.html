Where the wins come from

Every verified rule is an algorithm or protocol switch, never a channel tweak:

  • 3n 256K — default runs TREE/LL/48, config runs TREE/LL128/32. Protocol switch, +12.7%.
  • 3n 128M — default stays on RING/LL128, config moves to RING/LL128/40. +12.4%.
  • 3n 256M — default RING/LL128/48, config RING/SIMPLE/40. Protocol switch made earlier than the default makes it, +16.7%.
  • 2n 32M — TREE→RING, +10.8%.

That is the multi-node result in one line: RCCL's channel choices are already good at 2 and 3 nodes; its algorithm and protocol thresholds are not. At 1 node the reverse holds.

The 3-node default has a real dip at 128M. Bandwidth falls from 263.5 at 64M to 241.4 at 128M, then recovers to 294.5 at 256M — lower at twice the size. The config's 40 channels reach 271.4 with no dip. This reproduces the same anomaly seen on 2026-08-04, on a different allocation two weeks later.

Two wins lost to single stalled repeats

3n 32M measured +7.6% and 3n 128K measured +5.1% — both real gains, both dropped for P(sup) 0.86 and 0.92 against a 0.95 gate. The cause in each case is one stalled repeat out of seven:

Six repeats agree within 5%; one collapses by 16×. P(sup) is a rank statistic, so a single loss costs 7 of 49 pairings — enough to fall below the gate. The gate is behaving correctly: it is telling us the measurement was not reproducible, and it was not. Re-running those two sizes on a quieter machine would likely convert both.

Likely cause: a NIC-metrics poller (amd-nic-metrics) spawns 9 concurrent nicctl show rdma queue-pair statistics processes per node every ~30 s for ~9 s. Runs take 11–15 s, so roughly a third overlap a poll. See findings/05.

Validity

  • Preflight passed at both scales — 19.4% worst spread at 2 nodes, 22.3% at 3 nodes, both under the 25% limit, measured on 5 default runs before the A/B began.
  • 0 hangs in 28 measured runs.
  • Within-arm spread: 2 of 36 points over 25% at 2 nodes, 1 of 36 at 3 nodes — both far under the 20%-of-points threshold that would mark the run invalid.
  • Plugin firing verified in every per-rank INFO log (--plugin-must-fire).
  • 3 nodes needed 8 warm-up runs rather than 4; with 4 the preflight failed at 210% spread on a freshly allocated node.

Scope and limits

  • all_reduce only.
  • RCCL 2.28.3-develop:2e42aa8. The acceptance map is version-specific.
  • Config generated at 1% tolerance. The default has since moved to 0.5%, which selects slightly more channels at a few sizes; that variant is not yet A/B'd.
  • Node sets are the 2026-08-04 sets exactly — 2 nodes 5,7 (same L1 leaf), 3 nodes 5,6,7 (2×L1 + 1×L2) — so these numbers are comparable to that run.
